Rumus Inti

KMW = OMW + (SIDPP / (0.052 × TVD))
ICP = SIDPP + SCR
FCP = SCR × (KMW / OMW)
Hi-Vis Pill → Total kg = BBL × Dosis (ppb) × 0.4536
KCl (Target SG) → kg/L = (SG − 1) / (2.42 − SG)
KCl (Target %) → Lbs = BBL × 42 × PPG × (% / 100)
BF = (65.5 − MW) / 65.5
HP = 0.052 × MW × TVD
PG = 0.052 × MW
Force = Pressure × Area
Area = π/4 × (OD² − ID²)
Pipe capacity = ID² / 1029.4 bbl/ft
Annular capacity = (ID² − OD²) / 1029.4 bbl/ft
Bbl/stroke = 0.000243 × D² × L × Efficiency
